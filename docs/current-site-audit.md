## Signature methods

- **Femina Bella Est®**: Bella's own technique. Sculpting movements, meridian stimulation, lymphatic
  activation, acupressure, Gua Sha and vacuum. "A natural facelift effect."
- **Buccal massage**: intraoral, reaches the deep jaw and cheek muscles (masseter, temporalis, pterygoids).
- **Gua Sha sculpting**: traditional Eastern tool ritual for circulation, lymph flow, contour.
- Others named on Bokadirekt: Jaw Release, Symmetry Sculpt Facial™, Bojin Acupressure Lift™,
  Heart Space Gua Sha Release, Abdominal Sculpt™, Yngre Imorgon™ (a six-treatment journey).
