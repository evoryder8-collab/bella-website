# Sound effects

`click`, `magic`, `pages-riffle` and the five `whoosh-*` files are the user's own
recordings (their SFX library).

The rest are recordings from [Freesound](https://freesound.org), the library
[Openverse](https://openverse.org) indexes for audio. All are **CC0** (public
domain): free for commercial use, no attribution required; credited here anyway.
Each was trimmed, high-passed, made mono and peak-levelled to -1 dBFS. `air.mp3`
is a seamless 12 s loop with a quarter second of its own continuation either
side (loop between 0.25 s and 12.25 s).
